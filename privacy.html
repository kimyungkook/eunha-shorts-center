Privacy Policy

The app runs on your computer. Your data stays there.

Last updated: 26 August 2026

1. Summary

Eunha Shorts Center is a desktop application. It has no server of ours behind it. Everything it reads from TikTok is written to files on your own computer, and it talks to no one except TikTok's official API endpoints and, if you enable notifications, the notification service whose token you supplied.

2. What we access from TikTok

Only for the account you connect, and only within the scopes you approve:

user.info.basicOpen ID, display name, avatar. Used to show which account is connected and to attach uploads to the correct account.
user.info.statsFollower count, total likes, video count. Used for the growth chart.
video.listYour own video IDs, creation times, view, like and comment counts. Used for the per-video performance table.
video.publishUsed to upload the video file you selected and to check its publishing status. This scope reads nothing.

We never access other users' data, private messages, contacts, or any TikTok data outside these scopes.

3. Where data is stored

  • Access token — in a file in the app's config folder on your computer.
  • Statistics — in a local SQLite database file in the app's data folder.
  • Video files — in the folder you chose. The app reads them; it does not copy or move them.
  • Logs — in the app's logs folder, recording what was published and any errors.

None of this is transmitted to us. We operate no servers that receive your data and we have no access to your computer.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not use your data to train machine-learning models.
  • We do not store your TikTok password — you sign in on TikTok's own page.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking on this website. No cookies are set.

5. How long data is kept

For as long as you keep it. Nothing expires on our side because nothing is on our side. The access token is refreshed automatically while you keep the connection; it stops working once you revoke the app in TikTok.

6. How to delete your data

  1. Revoke access in TikTok TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions → remove Eunha Shorts Center. The stored token stops working immediately.
  2. Delete the local files Delete the app's config, data and logs folders, or simply delete the whole app folder. That removes every trace of the collected data.

Because we hold nothing, there is no deletion request to send us. If you would still like written confirmation, email scfjhk7302@gmail.com.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal data. Because all data collected by the app is already in your own possession on your own computer, you can exercise all of these yourself at any time. For anything else, contact us at the address below.

8. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it. Material changes will be described on this page.
