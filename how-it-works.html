How it works

From an empty folder to a published post, in six steps.

The flow

  1. Connect your TikTok account Open the dashboard and click Connect. TikTok's own authorization page opens in your browser, lists the permissions being requested, and you approve or cancel. On approval the app receives an access token and shows your handle.
  2. Put a finished video in your folder Any MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI or WebM file. The app notices it within a minute.
  3. Review the draft The app proposes a title and hashtags and plays the video back so you can check you picked the right file. Edit anything you want to change.
  4. Choose the privacy level The list is fetched from your own account, so you only see options TikTok allows for you. Set comments, Duet and Stitch here too, and mark the post if it is branded content or a paid partnership.
  5. Confirm and publish Tick the final consent box and press publish. The app uploads the file to TikTok and then polls the status endpoint until TikTok reports the post is complete.
  6. Check the result The dashboard shows the publish ID and marks the job complete. If TikTok returns an error, the reason is shown in plain language along with what to do about it.

Scheduling instead of publishing now

Steps 3 to 6 can run on a timetable. You set the times of day you want to post — for example 19:00 and 22:00 — and each new video is assigned to the next free slot. A background worker publishes what is due at those times. You can move a video to the front of the queue, change a slot, or cancel a queued post at any point before it goes out.

The worker starts automatically a few minutes after you log in to your computer, so the schedule keeps running without you opening anything.

What happens when something fails

Network problems and temporary TikTok errors are retried a few times with a growing delay. Errors that will not fix themselves — an expired token, a file that is too long for your account, a permission that has not been granted — stop the job and are shown on the dashboard with the specific step to take.

A failed post is never silently retried into a duplicate. The app tracks which files have already been published and skips them.
