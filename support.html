Common questions

Does the app post without asking me?

No. Every post needs your confirmation. When you use the timetable, you confirm the title, hashtags and privacy level when the video is queued; the app then publishes that approved post at the time you chose. Nothing is generated and posted unattended.

Do you store my TikTok password?

No. You sign in on TikTok's own page. The app only receives an access token, and that token is stored in a file on your computer.

Can it read or post to someone else's account?

No. The app can only act on the account that authorized it, and only within the scopes you approved.

Where are my videos stored?

In the folder on your computer that you chose. The app reads them from there and uploads them to TikTok. It does not copy them anywhere else.

How do I disconnect?

Open TikTok, go to Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions, and remove Eunha Shorts Center. Then delete the app's config folder to remove the stored token, and delete the app's folder to remove everything else.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no charge, no subscription and no in-app purchase.

Messages you may see

unaudited_client_can_only_post_to_private_accounts The app's TikTok integration has not been approved yet, so it can only post to a private account. Nothing is wrong with your setup.
scope_not_authorized A permission the app needs was not granted. Disconnect and connect again, and approve all the listed permissions.
The chunk size is invalid The video file could not be split correctly for upload. Re-export the video and try again.
Video duration exceeds the maximum Your account has a maximum video length. The dashboard shows both your video's length and your account's limit.
Access token expired The app refreshes tokens automatically. If it cannot, connect your account again from the settings tab.

Reporting a problem with content

If you believe a video published through this app infringes your rights or breaks TikTok's Community Guidelines, report it inside TikTok using the report button on the post, and email us at scfjhk7302@gmail.com so we can help.
